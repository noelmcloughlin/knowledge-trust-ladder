# Glossary

* [LOKF](lokf.md) - The Linked Open Knowledge Format, the semantic profile of OKF this bundle is written in, which binds every field to a public vocabulary so the bundle projects to RDF.
* [OKF](okf.md) - The Open Knowledge Format, Google's specification for a folder of Markdown concept files with YAML frontmatter, which LOKF profiles.
* [Knowledge bundle](knowledge-bundle.md) - The `.lokf/knowledge` directory - containing one-concept-per-file Markdown, carrying a semantic header, that is simultaneously human-readable documentation and a queryable graph.
* [Trust label](trust-label.md) - The plain words the skills use for how far a concept has been checked, derived from its frontmatter on every read and never stored.
* [The pen](the-pen.md) - The name for `knowledge-apply.sh`, the only way ktl-librarian writes the bundle: it applies the operations in `.lokf/patch.yaml`, stamps `generated`, keeps the index in step, and refuses a `human:` actor.
* [Docent](docent.md) - A museum's name for a guide, and the role that answers a reader's questions from the bundle, says how far each answer has been checked, and writes nothing in the bundle.
* [Desk](desk.md) - The library word for the place where a role does its work with the bundle: the registrar is `knowledge-apply.sh` at the librarian's desk, and `knowledge-report.sh` at the reader's and the curator's.
* [Registrar](registrar.md) - The person in a museum who keeps the collection's records, and Knowledge Trust Ladder's fifth role, which programs play: the `lokf` toolkit, the gate `knowledge-registrar.yaml`, the pen and the report script.
* [Sidecar](sidecar.md) - `.lokf/`, the folder beside a project's code that holds the bundle and the tooling that checks it, kept out of the project's build; also the role, and the ktl-sidecar skill, that installs it.
* [Accession](accession.md) - A museum's word for an item added to its collection, which the registrar keeps documented with its provenance filed; in the bundle, each concept added is one.
* [Librarian](librarian.md) - The role that binds a repository's knowledge into order, played by the ktl-librarian skill: it derives concepts with their sources, classifies and links them, audits, and hands off for review. It catalogues without vouching, and writes the bundle only through the pen.
* [Curator](curator.md) - The role that holds the scales, always a person: in the museum sense, the one who authenticates, weighs provenance and decides what goes on exhibit. The ktl-curator skill and the KTL Curator plugin are that person's assistants, and reach no verdict of their own.
* [Collection](collection.md) - A repository's scattered knowledge as Knowledge Trust Ladder keeps it: catalogued, authenticated and explained, with the trust in every claim left visible. Each item added to the collection is an accession.
* [Context layer](context-layer.md) - A governed layer between an organisation's sources and whoever consumes them, person or agent, that persists from one task to the next. One knowledge bundle is one repository's context layer, and a design sets out how bundles federate into one layer.
* [Trust ladder](trust-ladder.md) - Knowledge Trust Ladder's answer to who is responsible for the quality of the context: written into each concept, where it came from and how far it has been checked, as rungs a claim climbs from still a draft to confirmed by a person.
* [Workshop](workshop.md) - In the two-vault arrangement for Obsidian, the vault a person already has, in which nothing is moved or migrated. The bundle opens beside it as the exhibition, a small vault of its own, and the two never index the same file.
* [Exhibition](exhibition.md) - In the two-vault arrangement for Obsidian, the bundle opened as a small vault of its own: the checked part of what the workshop knows, which teammates, CI and agents read. The README's Exhibitions are the sites that show a repository's collection.
* [Doorway](doorway.md) - `knowledge_bundle`, the link at a host's root that ktl-sidecar creates onto the bundle, so the hidden `.lokf/knowledge` folder has a name a folder picker can see. Obsidian opens the bundle as a vault of its own through it.
* [Health line](health-line.md) - The one line `knowledge-report.sh` prints for the whole bundle, counting its concepts under seven trust labels, such as "Confirmed by a person: 3 of 40". It is derived on every read and never stored, and it is the number to watch over time.
* [Work list](work-list.md) - What `knowledge-report.sh worklist` prints for ktl-librarian before each refresh, from frontmatter and git, in paths and dates and nobody's words: the concepts whose sources moved since they were derived or last checked, the notes a person left and the librarian's own open questions. It also counts the reader feedback that waits.
* [LinkML](linkml.md) - The schema language LOKF is written in, created by the LinkML Community. LOKF's specification is a single LinkML schema, from which the JSON Schema, JSON-LD context, SHACL shapes and OWL ontology are generated, and a domain schema that extends it is written in LinkML too.
* [Domain schema](domain-schema.md) - A schema written in LinkML that extends LOKF's, for a domain whose concepts stop fitting LOKF's short list of classes, so the domain's own types are checked rather than tolerated. The curator flags the drift, the team decides, and the librarian applies it.
* [Three lines of defence](three-lines-of-defence.md) - The model regulated industries use to say who owns a risk, who keeps the rules and who checks independently, whose reference version is the IIA's Three Lines Model. In Knowledge Trust Ladder the librarian and the curator are the first line, the registrar the second, and the bundle holds the evidence a third line would need.
